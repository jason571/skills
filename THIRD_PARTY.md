Skill packages are distributed with their supplied licenses and notices. A package's instructions, scripts, fonts, graphics and model dependencies may have different terms; no collection-wide license replaces them.

| --- | --- |
| canvas-design, frontend-design, mcp-builder, skill-creator, theme-factory | Derived from [Anthropic Skills](https://github.com/anthropics/skills); preserve each included Apache-2.0 `LICENSE.txt` |
| canvas-design fonts | Keep each font's accompanying `*-OFL.txt` alongside its files |
| find-skills | Included MIT license; [Vercel Skills](https://github.com/vercel-labs/skills) ecosystem |
| Tavily packages | [Tavily Skills](https://github.com/tavily-ai/skills); preserve supplied per-package licenses and consult upstream for packages without one |
| minimax-h3-director | Included Apache-2.0 license and `NOTICE`; its brand graphic has separate ownership terms stated in that notice |
| Other task Skills | Preserve any package-specific terms; absence of a license does not imply a redistribution grant |

Local `docx`, `pptx`, `pdf` and `xlsx` packages are intentionally excluded from this repository. Their supplied [upstream license](https://github.com/anthropics/skills/blob/main/skills/docx/LICENSE.txt) restricts redistribution. No license was removed or replaced to include these packages. Obtain them directly from their publisher where permitted.

Model/service use is independent of Skill instructions. MiniMax, YuE2, image generation and hosted search services may have separate model licenses, quotas or commercial terms. Model weights, credentials and private generated outputs are not distributed here. Included Director demonstrations are documentation resources; design fonts remain with their license files.
